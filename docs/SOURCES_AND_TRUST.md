# Sources and trust register

## Observed source pair

A. Directorate of Immigration Services, Kenya
https://immigration.go.ke/application-requirements/
Section: Requirements for new (First time) applications.
Short excerpt: “2 payment invoices”.

B. eCitizen Immigration passport page
https://immigration.ecitizen.go.ke/index.php?id=4
Section: Steps of Application / During the Submission.
Short excerpt: “three application receipts”.

Both pages were accessed on 19 September 2026 through web retrieval and direct fetch.
`data/retrieval_receipts.json` contains URL, check date, byte count and HTML SHA-256.
Full source HTML is not redistributed. A hash identifies the retrieved bytes; it does
not establish truth or currency. Page publication and effective dates were not stated
in the relevant sections. Copyright footer years were not used as effective dates.

The wording difference is observed. Whether invoices and receipts are the same artifact,
whether both pages remain current, and local office applicability are unresolved.
The app therefore classifies the pair as terminology review. This is not a verified
institutional contradiction, legal ruling, or instruction to bring a particular count.

The scope is curated for first-passport preparation by an adult Kenyan citizen by birth.
The eCitizen page is broader, and this mapping itself needs local institutional review.
Matching identity-document statements are not a full list of application requirements.
Fees, photos, processing times and other uncertain or unreviewed requirements are not
included as action instructions. Users should consult the institution for the full process.

Institution contact email: info@immigration.go.ke, listed on source A. The app provides
an optional contact link and a draft; it does not send correspondence automatically.

## Synthetic cases

Five fixtures cover same-term count differences, different locations, different time
periods, a missing statement and agreement. Their documents and effective dates are
invented. They have no source URLs and are labeled in the interface and downloads.
The Before You Travel update demo is a separate fictional case created in browser
memory. It changes the receipt-copy count and never modifies the catalog or real save.

## Language, access and privacy

English and AI-drafted Swahili. Independent Swahili review is pending and shown in the
interface. Semantic controls, visible focus, text statuses and responsive layouts help
accessibility; no WCAG certification or human screen-reader study is claimed.

First use requires a browser and internet. Saved snapshots, downloads and printing
support intermittent connectivity and assisted sharing. SMS/USSD is not implemented.

There is no login, document upload, analytics or personal-data field. A saved comparison
and checkbox state are stored in localStorage, not encrypted. Clear saved data removes
the app's local snapshot, progress, application cache and service-worker registration.
It cannot remove downloads, printouts or browser history. The host may still process
IP addresses and normal request metadata; do not claim anonymity.

## Governance and review age

Thirty days is a prototype review interval, not an institutional standard. Overdue
checks remain visible. A maintainer reviews changes before publishing a new version.
No automatic authority ranking, rumor voting, latest-page-wins rule or truth score is used.

## Before You Travel integration, 20 September 2026

The integration preserves the existing source checks dated 19 September 2026; it does
not claim a fresh retrieval. Matching items form a partial preparation plan. The
fictional two-to-three receipt-copy update is isolated in browser memory and is not
new official evidence. Requirement changes reopen affected steps; check-date-only
updates do not. Unknown effective dates and office applicability stay unresolved.
